What I do

Rozafë Rexhepaj is a Vienna-based journalist who writes in Albanian, German and English. Since 2019 she has reported for Albinfo on integration, business and culture among Albanian-speaking communities in Austria and Switzerland. She learned her craft in Prishtina’s newsrooms, holds an MA in Communication Science from the University of Vienna, and leads teams.

  1. 01

    Research & interviews

    Finding sources, conducting interviews and checking facts, with people from politics, administration, business, academia and civil society.

  2. 02

    Multilingual content

    Profiles, features and guides in Albanian, German and English, online and in print.

  3. 03

    Community & public relations

    A network built up over years with the organisations, institutions and businesses of Austria’s Albanian-speaking community.

  4. 04

    Team leadership

    Leading shifts of up to 15 people, training new colleagues, keeping costs under control and reporting on them.
