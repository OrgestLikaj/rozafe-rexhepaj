Albinfo · profile series · 2022 – 2026

Making the diaspora visible

  • Interviewing
  • Source network
  • Profile writing
  • Albanian

The brief

People from the Albanian-speaking community in Austria and Switzerland whose careers are worth telling: in medicine, science, aviation, watchmaking and music.

Approach

  • Finding interviewees through a network of sources built up over several years.
  • Conducting in-depth interviews that draw out the personal path behind each career.
  • Writing in Albanian for readers online and in the print magazine.

Outcome

A series of published profiles, including an orthopaedic surgeon in Vienna, a researcher in Amsterdam, a helicopter pilot in Switzerland and a musician in Austria.

HAJDE · service journalism · 2025

Arriving in Austria

  • Service journalism
  • Research
  • Plain language
  • English

The brief

People moving to Austria from Albania, Kosovo, Serbia or Türkiye need reliable answers to practical questions. Which documents? Which insurance? What does the siren on Saturday mean?

Approach

  • Researching official requirements and turning them into clear, practical steps.
  • Answering the questions newcomers actually ask, such as why travel insurance is not enough for a residence permit.

Outcome

Three guides and news pieces in October 2025: documents for non-EU citizens, health insurance, and Austria’s nationwide civil-defence siren test.

Albinfo · integration · 2020 – 2021

Albanian students in Austria

  • Integration
  • Education
  • Interviews
  • Follow-up reporting

The brief

More than 2,000 Albanian students are enrolled at Austrian universities. Many face financial and bureaucratic hurdles on their own.

Approach

  • Letting students speak for themselves about money, paperwork and language as the key to integration.
  • Following up on what changed: the student association “Societas Albania”, which helps newcomers in Vienna.

Outcome

Two articles that put an overlooked group at the centre, and the self-help it organises.

University of Vienna · MA thesis · 2024

Why newspapers in Kosovo disappear

  • Media analysis
  • Media economics
  • Academic research

The brief

How did financial challenges and the COVID-19 pandemic influence the closure of newspapers in Kosovo?

Approach

  • Academic research for the MA in Communication Science.
  • Brings together first-hand newsroom experience in Kosovo and communication-science methods.

Outcome

Master’s thesis at the University of Vienna, completed in 2024 (written in German).
